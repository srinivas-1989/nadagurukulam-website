**HINDUSTANI VOCAL THEORY**

**Year 1: Structural Foundations**

**Semester 1: Fundamental Grammar**

- **Module 1: Basic Vocabulary :** Study of *Nada, Shruti, Swara
  (Shuddha/Vikrit), Saptak,* and the concept of *Laya*.

- **Module 2: Raga Characteristics :** *Vadi, Samvadi, Anuvadi, Vivadi,
  Varjya,* and *Vakra* swaras.

- **Module 3: Thaat & Time Theory :** The 10 Thaat system of Pt.
  Bhatkhande; Classification of Ragas by time (Purvanga/Uttaranga).

- **Module 4: Raga Lakshana :** Detailed study of 5 foundational Ragas
  (e.g., Yaman, Alhaiya Bilawal, Bhairav).

- **Methodology:** Lecture-demonstrations using the Harmonium/Tanpura
  for interval identification.

**Semester 2: Notation & Classical Forms**

- **Module 1: Notation Systems :** Mastery of Pt. V.N. Bhatkhande and
  Pt. V.D. Paluskar notation for melody and rhythm.

- **Module 2: Major Vocal Forms :** Structural study of *Dhrupad,
  Dhamar,* and *Khayal (Vilambit/Drut)*.

- **Module 3: Secondary Forms :** Definition and characteristics of
  *Tarana, Trivat, Chaturang,* and *Sargam Geet*.

- **Module 4: Early Treatises :** Overview of *Natyasastra* and
  *Dattilam* regarding Vocal music.

- **Methodology:** Notation workshops where students transcribe recorded
  *Bandishes*.

**Year 2: History & Rhythm**

**Semester 3: Historical Treatises**

- **Module 1: Ancient Era :** Study of Matanga's *Brihaddesi* and the
  emergence of the Raga concept.

- **Module 2: Medieval Era :** *Sangita Ratnakara* of Sharngadeva---an
  overview of the *Saptadhyayi*.

- **Module 3: Transition Period :** *Swaramela Kalanidhi* and the works
  of *Ahobala (Sangit Parijat)*.

- **Module 4: Institutionalization :** The role of music conferences and
  the contributions of Pt. Bhatkhande.

- **Methodology:** Comparative textual analysis and student-led seminars
  on historical shifts.

**Semester 4: The Tala System**

- **Module 1: Tala Fundamentals :** *Matra, Vibhag, Sam, Khali, Tali,*
  and the 10 *Pranas* of Tala.

- **Module 2: Major Talas :** Study of *Teental, Ektal, Jhaptal, Rupak,
  Chautal, Dhamar*.

- **Module 3: Layakari :** Mathematical notation of *Dugun, Tigun,
  Chaugun,* and *Ari/Kuad* (\$1.5\$ speed).

- **Module 4: Accompaniment Ethics :** Relationship between the vocalist
  and the Tabla player.

- **Methodology:** Rhythmic drills (clapping/recitation) and
  mathematical calculation of *Tihais*.

**Year 3: Style & Aesthetics**

**Semester 5: The Gharana System**

- **Module 1: Origin of Gharanas :** Socio-cultural factors and the
  transition from Dhrupad to Khayal.

- **Module 2: Major Khayal Gharanas :** *Gwalior, Agra, Kirana,
  Jaipur-Atrauli, Patiala*.

- **Module 3: Stylistic Differences :** Analyzing *Tan-patterns, Meend,*
  and *Kanas* across different schools.

- **Module 4: Biographies :** Life and work of Tansen, Sadarang-Adarang,
  and 20th-century pioneers.

- **Methodology:** Comparative listening using archival audio to
  identify stylistic markers.

**Semester 6: Aesthetics & Semi-Classical Forms**

- **Module 1: Rasa Theory :** The *Rasa Sutra* and its application in
  Hindustani performance.

- **Module 2: Semi-Classical Genres :** Aesthetics of *Thumri, Tappa,
  Dadra,* and *Bhajan*.

- **Module 3: Folk Influences :** Study of *Maand, Baul, and Bhatiyali*
  and their interaction with classical music.

- **Module 4: Concert Planning :** Principles of choosing Ragas based on
  time and audience dynamics.

- **Methodology:** Creative workshops on *Bhava* (expression) and guest
  artist interactions.

**Year 4: Research & Teaching Aptitude, Dissertation and UGC NET**

**Semester 7: Musicology, Teaching & Research Aptitude**

- **Module 1:** Research Methodology in Music

- **Module 2:** Teaching & Research Aptitude (Paper 1 Core)

- **Module 3:** Logical Reasoning & Higher Education System (Paper 1
  Core)

- **Module 4:** Musicological Research & Fieldwork Techniques

**Semester 8: Dissertation & UGC NET**

- **Module 1:** The Research Dissertation (Project Work).

- **Module 2:** Professional Music Management & Law.

- **Module 3:** Advanced Pedagogy & Practical Teaching.

- **Module 4:** UGC NET & JRF Intensive Review.

- **Methodology:** Mock NET exams and hands-on training with notation
  software.
